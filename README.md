# Wearable-Acoustic-Sensing Sleeve
**McGill MACRObotics Lab | Robotics Research | ICRA 2026**
Wearable acoustic waveguide sensing for contact localization and force estimation along robotic arms.

## Overview

This project explores the use of fabric-embedded acoustic waveguides for contact localization and force estimation in wearable sensing systems.

The goal is to develop a flexible, wearable sensing solution capable of detecting physical interactions along an extended surface.

## My Contributions

- Designed, fabricated, and iterated multiple wearable sleeve prototypes.
- Developed acoustic sensing and data acquisition systems.
- Conducted mechanical testing and collected experimental sensing data.
- Implemented signal processing techniques for analyzing acoustic reflections.
- Contributed to research published at ICRA 2026.

## Technical Approach

The system uses ultrasonic signals transmitted through flexible waveguides embedded in fabric.

By analyzing signal reflections, the system can estimate where contact occurs and characterize the applied force.
